## Full description
Dejoiy Mail is business email for your company, on your own domain, made to feel like an app.

MAIL IN COLOUR
Every label has its own colour: Important is pink, Work is yellow, Money is orange, Personal is green. Mail without a label gets a colour from what it says. Open a mail and it opens in the same colour.

WRITE FASTER
Pick a label while you write, or leave it on Auto. Mail you send arrives in Gmail and other inboxes as a clean card in the label colour.

CONTACTS THAT FILL THEMSELVES
Everyone you write to is saved automatically. People at your company show under Work family, everyone else under Others.

UPDATES FOR YOUR TEAM
Dejoiy Updates brings news and tips from the Dejoiy team. Your company box carries updates from your own company, with photos and files.

MAKE IT YOURS
Dejoiy colours, background themes, dark mode and your own profile photo.

BUILT FOR YOUR PHONE
Swipe, select, search and reply with one hand. Works on phones, tablets and computers.

Your mail is yours: we don't sell your data or show ads.

Dejoiy Mail needs a mailbox from your company. New company? Create one at mail.dejoiy.com/signup and try any plan free for 11 days.
