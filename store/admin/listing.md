## Full description
Dejoiy Mail Admin is the console for your company's Dejoiy Mail, on your phone.

MAILBOXES IN ONE TAP
Create a mailbox for a new hire, reset a password, set storage, add aliases and forwarding. Lock an account without deleting a single message.

RUN IT LIKE BUSINESS UNITS
Group people into departments, each with its own address like sales@yourcompany.com. Create roles and choose exactly what each role may do: post company updates, manage mailboxes, delete mailboxes, manage departments, see DNS, or manage roles.

YOUR COMPANY BOX
Name your company's update page and decide who can post there.

DNS, SORTED
MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC are checked live, with the exact records to copy so your mail stays out of spam folders.

SAFE BY DESIGN
The console asks for your password again before changes and locks after 15 minutes. Company admins only ever see their own company.

For company administrators of Dejoiy Mail. Not an admin? Get the Dejoiy Mail app instead.
