Writing a mail (Compose)

What it is: the page where you write and send a mail.

Where: the Compose button. On a phone, the round Compose button, or the pencil at the top in the Clean look. On a computer you can also press C.

How to use it:

  1. In To, start typing a name or an address. Up to 6 suggestions appear: people whose name or address starts with what you typed come first, then the people you mail most. Click into an empty To box to see the people you write to most.
  2. Use the arrow keys to move through the suggestions and Enter to pick one. Esc closes the list.
  3. Tap Cc/Bcc if you need those rows. Put people in Bcc when the others should not see them.
  4. Write a Subject and your message. The bar above the text has bold, italic, underline, strike, lists and links.
  5. Choose a Label (see below), add files with the paperclip if you need them, then tap Send.

When an address becomes a "chip"

A chip is the small rounded box an address turns into. Only chips get the mail.

  • Enter, comma or semicolon: what you typed becomes a chip.
  • Tab: makes a chip, but only if you typed something.
  • Space: makes a chip only when what you typed is a whole address (like asha@company.com). A half-typed address stays as text, so you can keep typing.
  • Leaving the box: a whole address is kept as a chip; half of one is not.
  • Pasting a list of addresses (separated by commas, semicolons or spaces) adds them all.
  • Backspace in an empty box removes the last chip. The × on a chip removes that one.
  • A chip with a wrong address is marked "This doesn't look like an email address", and Send will not work until you fix it.

"Often with"

After you add someone in To, Dejoiy Mail may show up to 4 buttons like + Ravi. These are people who were on the same mails as that person at least twice in your Sent mail. Each button shows To, Cc or Bcc: the box you usually used for them. Tap it to add them there.

Mailing a whole group

Type one of these in To (they also appear in the suggestions):

  • @all: everyone with an active mailbox in your company.
  • @dept:<department>: everyone in one department, for example @dept:customer-support for "Customer Support". The name is written in small letters with - instead of spaces. The suggestion shows how many people are in it.
  • @role:<designation>: everyone with that designation, written the same way, for example @role:team-lead.

The people in the group get the mail as Bcc, so they do not see each other's addresses. You are left out of the group. If To would be empty, your own address goes in To. If a group has nobody else in it, you see "Nobody else is in …" and the mail is not sent.

Labels

Under the subject you choose one label: ✨ Auto (the default: the app guesses from the subject and text), Important, Urgent, Work, Money, Personal, Ideas, or one of your own labels. The label decides the coloured band on the mail you send. It is also put on your copy in Sent. Important also marks the mail as important.

Attachments, drafts and undo

  • Up to 20 files per mail. The total size limit is set by the server (normally 15 MB). The × next to a file removes it.
  • Drafts save by themselves every 15 seconds and when you leave the page. You can also tap Save draft. Discard asks before it throws the mail away.
  • Undo Send: while "Sending…" shows, you can tap Undo. How long you get is set in Settings → General → Undo Send (Off, 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds).
  • Your signature is added by itself to new mails and above the quoted text in replies.
  • Templates: first switch them on in Settings → Advanced → Templates. Then Compose shows a Templates button to insert a saved text or save the current mail as a template.
  • Send & archive shows on replies when you switch it on in Settings → General.

Keep in mind: a group like @all can reach many people. Check the number in the suggestion before you send.

Reading mail

Coloured cards

What it is: each mail gets a colour that says what kind of mail it is: Important, Urgent, Security, Money, Work, Personal, Ideas or Updates. The open mail always shows as a card in that colour, with the kind written on a small tag.

How the colour is chosen: first a mail marked important, then mail in Spam, then the mail's labels, then a guess from the text (codes and sign-ins, money, work, newsletters).

The code card (OTP)

What it is: when a mail contains a one-time code (for example "Your verification code is 482913"), Dejoiy Mail shows the code on its own.

Where: in the list, a chip Copy code: 482913. In the open mail, a card Code requested with the code and a Copy code button.

How to use it: tap Copy code. You see "Code copied." If copying does not work, the message shows the code so you can type it.

Keep in mind: never give a code to anyone who asks for it, even if they say they are from your company. Codes are not looked for in Sent and Drafts.

Phone look: Clean or Colourful

Where: Settings → General → Phone app look.

  • Clean (the default): a calm white inbox, with Delete, Archive, Reply, Forward and More at the bottom while you read a mail. The aA button changes the text size.
  • Colourful: bright cards, one colour per kind of mail.

Theme background on phone

Where: Settings → General → Theme background on phone.

  • Off (the default): a plain screen on your phone.
  • On: your theme's picture shows behind your mail. This is for phones only; your computer keeps its own setting.

Panel transparency

What it is: how much of the theme's picture shows through the panels, from 0% to 85%.

Where: the account menu → Themes, or Settings → Themes. The slider only shows for themes with a picture behind them.

How to use it: move the Panel transparency slider. On a phone it is called "Panel transparency on phones" and it is saved apart from your computer's setting. If the slider is missing on your phone, first turn on Settings → General → Theme background on phone.

Keep in mind: high transparency can make text harder to read on a busy picture. Lower it if text looks faint.

Working with a mail

  • At the top of an open mail: Reply, Reply all, Forward, Archive, Spam, Delete, Star, Mark unread, Print, and More (which also has Block for the sender).
  • Unsubscribe shows next to the sender when the mail has an unsubscribe link.
  • Tap an address to Copy address, see Emails with that person, or Write to them.
  • On a phone: swipe a mail in the Inbox to archive it (with Undo), press and hold to select several, and pull down to refresh.
  • Search: type in the search box at the top. Press Enter for a results page. Advanced search lets you search by From, To, Subject, words, attachments and dates.
  • Keyboard keys (computer): see Settings → Keyboard shortcuts.

Several accounts

What it is: stay signed in to up to 5 Dejoiy Mail mailboxes in the same browser and switch between them.

Where: account menu → ＋ Add another account.

How to use it:

  1. Open the account menu and tap ＋ Add another account.
  2. Enter the other mailbox's Email address and Password, then Sign in.
  3. To switch, open the account menu and tap 🔁 Switch to … for the account you want. The page reloads with that mailbox.
  4. ↩ Sign out of this account signs out only the open one.

Keep in mind: with 5 accounts signed in you must sign out of one before adding another. On a company's own sign-in page you can only add that company's mailboxes.

Help desk (not yet tested on live)

What it is: a place to ask a team in your company for help (IT, HR, payroll, your admins…) and follow the request until it is done. Each request is a ticket with a code like T-0007.

Where: the left menu → Help desk.

Raising a ticket

  1. Open Help desk and tap New ticket.
  2. What do you need? Write a short title, for example "Laptop will not start".
  3. Which team should handle it? Choose one of your company's departments, or Company admin (your company's mail admins). Company admins also see Dejoiy platform (the Dejoiy team).
  4. How urgent is it? Choose a priority (see the table).
  5. Details: what happened, since when, and what you already tried.
  6. Tap Send ticket. The ticket opens, and you see it under My tickets.
PriorityThe team should answer within
Urgent4 hours
High8 hours
Normal (default)24 hours
Low72 hours

The time counts from when the ticket was raised. A ticket that is past its time and not finished shows Overdue.

What the status means

StatusMeaning
OpenRaised, nobody has started on it yet.
In progressSomeone in the team is working on it.
Waiting for youThe team needs an answer from you. (The team sees this as "Waiting for requester".)
ResolvedThe team thinks it is done.
ClosedFinished.

Answering, closing and reopening

  1. Open the ticket from My tickets.
  2. Write in the box under the messages and tap Send. If the ticket was Waiting for you, your answer sends it back to the team.
  3. If you do not need it any more, tap Close: I no longer need this.
  4. If a resolved or closed ticket is not really solved, tap Reopen: this is not solved.

You get a short mail in your Inbox titled Help desk: T-0007 · … when someone answers you or changes the status. Nobody gets a mail when a ticket is first raised.

If you work in the team that handles tickets

  • Your queue is the second tab: <your department> queue. For company admins it is Company admin queue (or <department> + Company admin queue). The number shows how many wait.
  • On a ticket you can set Status, Assigned to, Priority and Team (to move it to another team). When you set In progress or Resolved on a ticket with nobody assigned, it is assigned to you.
  • Tick Team only before Send to write a note the person who raised it never sees.
  • Company admins, and roles with "See and handle every help desk ticket", also have All company tickets.

Keep in mind: only the person who raised a ticket, the team it is for, the person it is assigned to and the company's admins can see it. Moving a ticket to another team can take it out of your own view.

Report a bug (not yet tested on live)

What it is: a quick way to tell the people who can fix it that something in Dejoiy Mail does not work.

Where: account menu → 🐞 Report a bug, or Help desk → Report a bug. The Report a bug button in this guide opens the same form.

How to use it:

  1. Open Report a bug. A New ticket form opens with the title "Bug: " already filled in.
  2. Finish the title, for example "Bug: attachments do not open on my phone".
  3. In Details write what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Add the page you were on and your phone or browser.
  4. Tap Send ticket.

It goes to your Company admin. If you are a company admin, it goes to the Dejoiy platform team. You can follow it in Help desk → My tickets.

Keep in mind: do not send bugs by mail. A ticket is tracked until it is fixed; a mail can get lost.

Joi, the AI buddy

What it is: Joi is Dejoiy's AI assistant.

Where: the Joi button at the top, account menu → ✨ Ask Joi, your AI buddy, or the keys Ctrl+J (Cmd+J on a Mac).

How to use it:

  1. Open Joi. A chat panel opens next to your mail.
  2. Ask your question. Joi sees the mail you have open and your newest mails (up to 15 from the Inbox and 5 from Sent), so you can ask things like "what does this mail want from me?".
  3. To bring Joi into a mail conversation, type @Joi in the text of your mail. Joi is then added in Cc.

Mails written by Joi carry an AI badge and the line "Joi is an AI. It can make mistakes."

Keep in mind: Joi can make mistakes. Check what Joi says before you act on it. Joi can read your mailbox for 12 hours after you open it; signing out ends that. (How and when Joi answers a mail is decided by Joi's own service, not described here.)

Calendar

Where: the left menu → Calendar.

How to use it:

  1. Choose Agenda or Month. Today jumps back to today.
  2. Tap Event to add one. Fill in the times, Repeat, Reminder, Location or video link (or tick Add a Shine video meeting), Guests and Notes, then Add event.
  3. Guests get an invitation by mail ("Saved. Invites sent.").

Booking link

  1. Tap Booking link and switch on People can book time with me.
  2. Set the Meeting length, the Days, the From and To hours, how much Notice you need, how far ahead people may Book up to, and a Break after each meeting.
  3. Copy or Share your link. People pick a day and time, enter their name and email, and tap Book it.

Keep in mind: anyone with your booking link can book a time, so share it only where you want bookings.

Contacts

Where: the left menu → Contacts.

  • + Add a contact, or search with Search contacts….
  • Import CSV and Export CSV move contacts in and out.
  • Contacts are grouped as Work family, Others and Other companies.

Filters and blocked addresses

What it is: rules that sort new mail by themselves, and a list of senders you never want to hear from.

Where: Settings → Filters and blocked addresses.

How to use it:

  1. Tap Create a new filter, say which mail it is for and what should happen, and save it.
  2. Pause a filter to stop it for a while, Resume to start it again, or Delete it.
  3. To block someone, add their address under Blocked addresses and tap Block. You can also block a sender from an open mail: More → Block.

Keep in mind: a filter only acts on mail that arrives after you save it.

Other places in the app

  • Home: a greeting, your unread mail, and what is new from Dejoiy and your company.
  • Dejoiy Updates and Company: news from Dejoiy and from your company. Only people allowed by their role can post in Company.
  • Chat: the Chat bubble (or the Chat tab on a phone) opens Shine chat next to your mail, when your company uses Shine.
  • Your status: the status line in the account menu sets you as available, busy, away or offline (when Shine is connected).
  • Profile photo: account menu → 📷 Change profile photo.
  • Admins: see the Admin guide.
