In short

  • We keep your mail so you can use it. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it for ads.
  • Your company decides who has mailboxes; we process mailbox data on its behalf.
  • Outside mail is delivered through Amazon Web Services; servers are run by our hosting provider.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and complain to our Grievance Officer.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Your company's mailboxes: the company that signed up decides who gets a mailbox and how it is used. For mail, contacts and files in those mailboxes, your company is the Data Fiduciary and Dejoiy processes the data on its behalf, as a Data Processor.

Our own records: for sign-up details, billing, support conversations and the security of our service, Dejoiy India Private Limited is the Data Fiduciary.

3. How we use it

  • To run your mailboxes: store, send, receive, search and show your mail and contacts.
  • To deliver mail to other providers, and to sign it (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so it isn't marked as spam.
  • To keep accounts and the service safe: detect spam, phishing, malware, misuse and unusual sign-ins.
  • To verify your domain, set up your company and bill you.
  • To help when you contact support, and to post service notices in Dejoiy Updates.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as tax records and lawful requests from authorities.

We rely on your company's agreement to our terms, the consent you give at sign-up, and the legitimate uses the law allows. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it; that may mean we can't keep providing the mailbox.

4. What we never do

  • We don't sell or rent personal data.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't scan your mail to target ads.
  • We don't read your mail, except when you or your admin ask for help with it, to stop abuse or a security threat, or when the law requires it.

6. Where data is kept

Data is stored on our hosting provider's servers and processed by Amazon Web Services, which may be located outside India. When data is transferred outside India, we do so as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules allow, and require our providers to protect it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Mailbox content is kept while the mailbox exists. Mail you delete goes to Trash and is then removed.
  • When a mailbox or company account is closed, its data is deleted 30 days later, after the export window in our terms.
  • Sign-in and mail delivery logs are kept for up to one year for security, or longer where the law requires.
  • Billing records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

8. Security

  • Connections to Dejoiy Mail are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Your sign-in is held in a secure, HTTP-only cookie that page scripts can't read.
  • Passwords are checked by the mail server and never stored in the web app.
  • The admin console asks for the admin's password again, and locks after 15 minutes.
  • Company admins can only manage their own company's domain.

No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell the affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete, update or delete it;
  • withdraw consent you gave us;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't act;
  • complain to our Grievance Officer and, if you aren't satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

For data in a company mailbox, start with your company's admin, who controls that mailbox. We will help them answer your request.

11. Children

Dejoiy Mail is a business service and isn't meant for children under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's data; if you think a child's data is in the service, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our service or the law changes. For important changes we will tell admins by email or in Dejoiy Updates before they apply. The date at the top shows the latest version.
