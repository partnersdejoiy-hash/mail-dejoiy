Run the whole company from here

Every item is a page in the console. The Admin guide inside it explains each one.

  • Templates

    Onboard, offboard, suspend or reactivate someone from one form. You see every step it took.

  • Departments and roles

    Give each department its own address, and each role its own permissions: who adds mailboxes, who posts to the company, who handles tickets.

  • Help desk tickets

    Tickets your people raise reach the right team. Open ones nobody picked up come to you, and you can raise one to the Dejoiy team.

  • Your own mail service

    Send outside mail through Amazon SES, Resend or any SMTP service your company already uses.

  • Your brand

    Your name and logo in every mailbox, a picture behind the sign-in form, and sign-in at your own address.

  • Policies

    Password rules, lockouts, forwarding and how long Trash and Spam are kept, per group of mailboxes.

  • Webhooks, API tokens, automations

    Tell your own tools when mail arrives or someone signs in, and switch on ready-made automations.

  • Undelivered mail and reports

    See every mail that did not arrive, with the reason, and who signed in or changed what.
